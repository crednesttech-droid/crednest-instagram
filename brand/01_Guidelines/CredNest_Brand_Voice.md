# CredNest — brand voice & Instagram content system

Version: 1.0, prepared 4 October 2026. Working v1 for the new brand kit. Source-grounded company statements are distinguished from new creative recommendations. This document proposes a usable voice; it does not claim that an older approved voice guide exists.

## Positioning and core message

**Brand name:** CredNest. Use this exact casing; do not split it into Cred Nest or change it to CREDNEST in body copy. The all-capital treatment may be used as a visual label.

**Descriptor:** Debt Advisory & Capital Solutions.

**Source line:** Independent advice. Lender-led decisions.

**Primary audience:** Indian startup founders and SME owners/operators with business capital needs. Finance teams are a useful secondary audience. This audience priority is a proposed interpretation of the profile, not research into actual follower demographics.

**Positioning:** CredNest helps Indian startups and SMEs define their debt-capital requirement, structure the facility, prepare credit information and coordinate engagement with independent lending partners.

**Proposed everyday explanation:** We help businesses turn a funding requirement into a clearer lender conversation.

**One-sentence value proposition:** Clearer capital requirements, structured credit preparation and coordinated lender engagement for Indian businesses.

**Brand promise:** Clarity and support through the process. Promise the work CredNest performs; do not promise a lender's decision.

**Proposed short campaign line:** Capital, with clarity.

Use the source line as the primary brand anchor. The campaign line is optional creative copy, not a claimed existing slogan.

**Source basis:** Company Profile 2026, pp. 1–3, 13–16. Messaging confidence: Medium; documented in one profile, not validated across customer research or live sales conversations.

## Personality: the expert guide

CredNest should feel like a prepared, thoughtful adviser who understands the business context, asks useful questions and explains the next step plainly. The personality below is inferred from the profile rather than presented as an already approved style guide.

Each attribute is a writing instruction. Together they create a calm, capable business brand. Avoid fear about a business failing, exaggerated urgency, flashy wealth language and slang that makes financial support feel careless.

## Voice constants

- Put the business need before the product label. Example: “Waiting on customer payments?” before introducing invoice financing.
- Explain one idea at a time. Give technical abbreviations in full on first use.
- Use short active sentences. Prefer “Prepare a clear credit pack” to “Facilitate comprehensive documentation readiness.”
- Be confident about CredNest's support and precise about lenders' decisions.
- Use second person for Instagram: your business, your requirement, your next step.
- Prefer concrete nouns: inventory, supplier payments, equipment, cash flow, documents, lender assessment.
- Avoid “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “unlock unlimited growth,” “best in India,” and unsupported “fastest” or “lowest.”
- Do not imply that “AI-assisted” means automated sanction, preferential approval, accuracy guarantees or proprietary underwriting. The profile does not establish those capabilities.

## Tone by context

Voice remains clear, disciplined and helpful. Energy, formality and detail change with context.

| Context | Formality | Energy | Detail | Example |

| Instagram service / lead post | Medium | Direct | State the support and one next step | “Define the requirement. Prepare the information. Engage relevant lenders.” |
| Founder story / educational reel | Low–medium | Warm | Business situation first | “Your capital requirement starts with what the money needs to do.” |

## English and optional Hinglish

**Default:** Plain Indian English. Use “₹” for verified rupee values and Indian numerical units consistently. Use “lakh” and “crore” in narrative copy; use “Cr” only when space is limited. Use “financing,” “programme” only where relevant, and consistent Indian English spelling throughout a document.

**Optional Hinglish:** Occasional audience-friendly hooks, while retaining finance terms and disclosures in English. Keep it natural and readable; use Roman script unless a Hindi-only post is deliberately planned. This is a creative recommendation, not a verified current brand policy.

Recommended examples:
- “Business grow ho raha hai. Capital plan clear hai?”
- “Funding ki need clear hai? Agla step: structure.”
- “Pehle requirement samjho. Phir facility discuss karo.”

Keep the body substantive and businesslike. Do not use “paisa pakka,” “loan guaranteed,” “tension-free approval,” or “bas DM karo, loan mil jayega.” Do not default to “bro” in public brand posts just because it is used in this working conversation.

## Instagram copy and layout rules for designers

A post should contain one headline, one core insight and one next action. Design supporting text to be readable on a phone.

- Cover headline: approximately 4–9 words, ideally two or three short lines.
- Supporting line: one short sentence; no paragraph on a cover.
- Carousel: five to seven slides is a useful starting convention. One idea per slide; the last slide contains one CTA and any required qualification.
- Body slides: one clear heading and two or three short points, with enough space for comfortable reading.
- Caption: a short opening hook, two or three useful sentences, one CTA, then the financing qualification when relevant.
- Reel: open with the business situation; explain one point; end with one practical action. Keep essential on-screen claims and subtitles legible.
- CTA: choose one of save, share, comment or contact. Do not ask for all four at once.
- Hashtags: select a small relevant set, such as #CredNest #DebtAdvisory #BusinessFinance #SMEFinance #StartupFinance; use only those relevant to that post. These are suggested labels, not a performance guarantee.
- Enquiries: invite a high-level business discussion. Do not ask people to publish PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements, account numbers or financial documents in comments or social DMs. Documentation belongs in the agreed private process.
