Data deletion instructions

To delete any information CredNest holds about you from your interactions with @crednest.io or the CredNest CRM app:

  1. Email contact@crednest.io with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram username, or send the same request by Instagram DM to @crednest.io.
  2. We delete the related records within 7 days and confirm by reply.

You can also remove the app's access at any time in Instagram: Settings, then Website permissions or Apps and websites.
