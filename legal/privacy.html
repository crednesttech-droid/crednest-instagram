CREDNEST ADVISORY

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

CredNest Advisory ("CredNest", "we") is a debt-capital facilitator based in Mumbai, India. We are not a lender or NBFC. This policy explains what we do with information when you interact with our Instagram account @crednest.io and our Facebook Page, including through our automated tools (the "CredNest CRM" app).

What we collect

  • When you comment on our posts: the comment ID, comment text and your Instagram username, as provided by Instagram.
  • When you send us a direct message: the conversation and message IDs, the message content and your Instagram user ID, as provided by Instagram.
  • If you contact us on WhatsApp after that: the details you choose to share with us there.

How we use it

  • To reply to your comment or message, for example by sending the free credit-pack checklist you asked for or our WhatsApp contact link.
  • To avoid replying to the same comment or message twice.
  • To answer your question about business, home or personal loans. Any decision on a loan is made only by the lender.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising profiles, or share it with lenders without your explicit consent.

What we store and for how long

Our automation keeps only opaque comment and message IDs, the type of reply sent and the time, so it does not reply twice. It does not store usernames or message text. These records are deleted after 90 days. Message content stays in Instagram's own inbox.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any information about you at any time. See Data deletion, or email contact@crednest.io.
